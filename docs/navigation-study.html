UX research · Navigation & app shell

SL-auto Navigation Study

How the app's navigation can become smoother, more intuitive and more refined — grounded in what the code and screenshots show today, and in published, proven patterns from Linear, Atlassian, Stripe, Material 3, Apple HIG, GOV.UK, Carbon and Nielsen Norman Group research.

Scope app shell, sidebar, header, tabs, breadcrumb, dossier page, lists, mobile, motion, visual tokens Codebase Next 15 · shadcn/ui · Tailwind 3 · SL-auto-main/SL-auto-main (dev folder) Date 31 Aug 2026

Summary — the eight moves that matter most

The shell is structurally sound (persistent sidebar, breadcrumb, workspace tabs, scroll-spied stepper, skeletons) but it is under-used: the header is empty, search and notifications exist as dead code, the same destination carries three different names, the primary action moves from page to page, and the dossier page stacks ~200 px of sticky chrome. None of the fixes require a re-skin; they are mostly consolidation and a few well-known patterns.

01 · quick win

One name per destination

Sidebar, breadcrumb, H1, tab and document.title read from one source (nav-groups.ts). "Gestion des dossiers / Dossiers / Files" becomes one word.

02 · quick win

A shared PageHeader

Breadcrumb → title (one size) → primary action right → filters/tabs below. Applied to all 19 pages; today titles range from text-base to text-4xl.

03 · quick win

Fill the header's right side

Search trigger (⌘K), "+ Nouveau dossier", rappels bell, user menu (theme, langue, déconnexion). Frees the sidebar footer.

04 · quick win

Quiet the sidebar

Tinted active row instead of solid teal fill, drop shadow-xl, fix the dead sub-item active state, move company CRUD out of the nav.

05 · structural

⌘K as a real navigation layer

Jump to a dossier by ref / plaque / assuré, go to any page, run actions, see recents. Plus g-chords and a ? shortcut sheet.

06 · structural

Stepper with real status

Per-step À faire / En cours / Terminé / Bloqué computed from data, not from position; stamp as helper text; no horizontal clipping.

07 · structural

One sticky record bar

Ref · assuré · plaque · statut · two actions, plus the stepper — replacing three stacked sticky bars on the dossier page.

08 · structural

Phone shell for agents

Bottom navigation bar (3–4 destinations), camera FAB, bottom sheets — no hamburger. Formalises the existing "Phone view".

Principle behind every recommendation: the sidebar is where product navigation lives; the top bar is for universal actions (search, create, notifications, profile); the page header owns the title, its one primary action and its filters. Atlassian rebuilt its navigation across six products on exactly this split after testing with 160+ users, and Linear, Stripe, Attio and Slack converge on it.

What already works — keep it

Several things in the shell are ahead of most back-office apps. They are the foundation the rest of this study builds on, not things to replace.

  • Persistent, collapsible sidebar with icon tooltips and ⌘B toggle (shadcn) — a "combo" nav, which NN/g measured as fast as fully visible nav.
  • Role-scoped nav with grant/deny overrides and role-aware landing (role-landing.ts).
  • Applied-filter chips with per-chip removal and reset — the pattern Baymard found missing on 28 % of sites.
  • Persisted list filters (usePersistedFilters) so a round-trip to a dossier doesn't lose context.
  • Skeleton loaders in 25 components and loading.tsx on the four heaviest routes.
  • Scroll-spied step bar on the dossier page with smooth scroll-to-section — the USWDS "in-page navigation" pattern.
  • Historique in a side sheet rather than a modal (NN/g: don't obscure the record).
  • Status badges as tinted background/foreground pairs, and a semantic token layer with a full dark palette.
  • Field-agent "Phone view" with card rows instead of a squeezed table.
  • Sticky rappel-session banner that states system status and pending change count.
01

Sidebar

The sidebar has the right bones (grouped, collapsible, tooltips). Its problems are weight, mixed responsibilities and labels.

The active item shouts, and the whole rail competes with the content

Impact · mediumEffort · small
ObservedActive item is a solid teal fill with white text; the sidebar carries shadow-xl z-50; group labels are 10 px; the brand block, theme toggle, user card and logout all sit in the rail. In the screenshots the sidebar is visually heavier than the page content.
EvidenceLinear's 2024 refresh made the sidebar "a few notches dimmer, allowing the main content area to take precedence" and softened borders ("structure should be felt, not seen"). Radix's 12-step scale reserves steps 4–5 for hover/selected surfaces and step 11–12 for text — a tinted row, not a solid accent. NN/g still requires the current location to be "distractingly obvious" to the designer — so keep the contrast, move it from fill to text weight + tint + a 2 px left bar. Linear · Radix · NN/g
Recommendation
  • Active: bg-sidebar-accent text-sidebar-accent-foreground font-medium + before: 2 px accent bar; hover one step lighter.
  • Remove the rail shadow; use the 1 px sidebar-border only. Sidebar background one step below the page.
  • Group labels ≥ 11.5 px, sentence case or small caps, muted.
  • Icons 16–18 px stroke, muted by default, accent only on active.
Why it feels smootherThe eye lands on the work area first; the rail becomes a stable frame. The same trick makes the dossier tab strip and page header read as one system.

Company management lives inside the navigation

Impact · mediumEffort · small
Observed"Compagnies" is a collapsible with every company as a sub-link, an inline "Ajouter" input, and a hover-revealed delete ✕ that opens a destructive confirm — all inside the nav. Sub-item active state checks pathname.includes('selected='), but usePathname() never contains the query string, so no sub-item ever highlights.
EvidenceApple HIG: sidebars show at most two levels and are for navigation, not editing. Vercel's Geist guideline caps a menu around 10 items and moves secondary actions to a settings page. NN/g: destructive actions need distance from routine ones.
HIG · Geist
Recommendation
  • Make "Compagnies" a plain link. Create/delete happen on /compagnies, where they already exist.
  • If per-company shortcuts are valuable, expose them as favourites (pinnable) rather than the full list — and read useSearchParams() for the active check.
BonusRemoves ~120 lines and an AlertDialog from the sidebar, and two Firestore hooks from every page render.

Labels are long, groups are vague, one group holds a single utility

Impact · mediumEffort · small
Observed"Assignations Agent de Terrain" wraps at the 16 rem rail; "Gestion des dossiers" vs page H1 "Dossiers"; group "ESPACE" carries no information scent; "DIVERS" exists for "Signaler un bug" alone. No recents or favourites for users who open 5–20 dossiers a day.
EvidenceNN/g: labels must be "information-scent-heavy"; hidden or vague labels cut discoverability by 20 %+. Atlassian's research produced Starred + Recent sections because users asked for frequent-item discovery; Stripe added a "Shortcuts" section of pinned + recent pages; Notion folded Recents/Favorites into the sidebar. Linear puts "Help & feedback" at the very bottom, outside the nav groups.
NN/g · Atlassian · Stripe
Recommendation
  • Groups: Opérations (Tableau de bord, Suivi d'équipe, Dossiers, Rappels, Consultation, Compagnies) · Assignations (Chiffrage, Terrain) · Administration (Utilisateurs, Tampons, Jours fériés).
  • Labels ≤ 2 words; the group already says "Assignations".
  • Add a Récents group (last 5 dossiers, from the existing tab store) — this is the single biggest speed-up for gestionnaires.
  • "Signaler un bug" moves into a footer help menu with the shortcut sheet.
NoteRole gating, grant/deny overrides and landingPathFor keep working unchanged — only label strings and group membership move.

Header & page header

The top bar is 56 px of breadcrumb and nothing else

Impact · highEffort · small
Observedheader.tsx renders a sidebar trigger (mobile) and the breadcrumb. global-search.tsx (cmdk, ⌘K, an AI "suggestion" box) and layout/notifications.tsx (bell + dropdown) exist but are imported nowhere. There is no create button, no user menu; theme and logout live in the sidebar footer.
EvidenceAtlassian: the top bar is "consistently dedicated to universal actions like search and create". NN/g on complex apps praises Power BI for a "+" that is "always positioned in the upper-right corner with uniform visual design across all workspaces". Attio and Slack put profile/settings/sign-out in one control, not spread across the rail.
Atlassian · NN/g
RecommendationRight cluster, in this order: Search (button showing ⌘K) · + Nouveau (role-aware: dossier / mission / rappel) · Rappels bell with unread count (wire the existing component to useRappels) · Avatar menu (nom + rôle, Mode sombre, Langue, Mon appareil, Déconnexion).
ResultSidebar footer shrinks to the collapse toggle and help; every page gains a constant "create" and "find" spot, which is the definition of consistency (NN/g H4).

03

Command palette & keyboard

For a team that opens the same kind of record dozens of times a day, keyboard access is the single most effective "smoothness" feature. The infrastructure (cmdk, ⌘K listener) is already in the repo; it just searches the wrong thing.

⌘K should navigate, not suggest

Impact · highEffort · medium
ObservedThe palette calls getAiSuggestion() after a 500 ms debounce and shows one sentence; selecting it closes the dialog. Empty state says "Start typing to search." (English). It is not mounted anyway.
EvidenceVercel Geist: the command menu holds resources (jump to a record), actions and navigation; shows recents when empty; nests into pages beyond ~30 items; shows each item's shortcut in a Kbd slot; traps and restores focus. Raycast made fuzzy matching its most-requested feature and ranks by frecency. Linear: "⌘K does anything by name".
Geist · Raycast
Recommendation
  • Groups: Récents (open tabs + last visited) · Dossiers (prefix query on refExpert, matricule via normalizePlate, assuré name) · Aller à (every visible nav item) · Actions (Nouveau dossier, Nouvelle planification, Envoyer un rappel, Mode sombre).
  • Show ↵ to open, ⌘↵ to open in a new workspace tab.
  • Keep the AI answer as an optional last row ("Demander à l'assistant…"), not the default result.
Data noteFirestore prefix queries need >=/<= on an indexed field; a small client-side index of the user's scoped dossiers (already loaded on the list page) covers most searches instantly.

04

Workspace tabs

The browser-style tab strip is a genuinely good fit for a claims desk (Zendesk, Freshdesk and every IDE use it). It currently behaves like a prototype of the pattern rather than the pattern.

Two copies, a lonely tab, and no tab semantics

Impact · mediumEffort · small–medium
Observeddossier-tabs-bar.tsx and chiffrage-tabs-bar.tsx are the same 90 lines twice. The strip renders even when only the permanent list tab exists (a 40 px band with one tab — visible in the Dossiers screenshot). Tabs have no unsaved-changes dot, no overflow menu (only native horizontal scroll), no ⌘W/⌘Tab, no middle-click, no reorder. Tab label is the insured's name while breadcrumb/H1 use the ref — two identities for one record. The strip disappears when you leave /dossiers, so open work vanishes from view.
EvidenceZendesk ticket tabs: overflow menu when tabs exceed width, drag reorder, per-tab colour dot with blue = unsaved changes, tab shows requester + ID. WinUI TabView (the most complete published spec): always one active tab, closing the active tab selects its neighbour, CtrlTab cycles, CtrlW closes, Ctrl1–8 jumps, keep a "reopen closed" history. VS Code's preview tabs (italic, reused until edited) prevent tab explosion.
Zendesk · WinUI · VS Code
Recommendation
  • One generic <WorkspaceTabs kind="dossier"|"chiffrage"> over a shared store.
  • Hide the strip until a second tab exists; keep it mounted across all routes (dim when not on the section).
  • Label = REF · Assuré, matching breadcrumb and H1; tooltip shows compagnie + plaque.
  • Unsaved dot from the rappel draft buffer / form dirty state; confirm on close when dirty.
  • Overflow "▾ n autres" menu, middle-click close, ⌘W, ⌘1–9, reopen-closed; optional pin.
  • Row single-click = preview tab (replaced by the next preview); double-click or edit = pinned tab.
FeelUsers stop losing track of what is open, the tab strip stops stealing 40 px on every list page, and the "which dossier is this?" mismatch disappears.

Wayfinding & labels

The breadcrumb hides the one thing users need: which record

Impact · mediumEffort · small
ObservedID-like segments are filtered out, so a dossier page reads "SL-auto › Gestion des dossiers" with no record crumb. The root crumb duplicates the sidebar's home link. Labels are a second, hand-maintained map (ROUTE_LABELS) that already drifts from NAV_GROUPS. The breadcrumb is hidden below md, leaving the mobile header empty.
EvidenceNN/g's breadcrumb guidelines: show hierarchy (not history), include the current page as a distinct last item, every crumb links to a real page; skip breadcrumbs on hierarchies only 1–2 levels deep; on mobile use a single "up one level" crumb with ≥ 1 cm targets. Baymard: pair hierarchy crumbs with a "back to list" affordance that preserves filters.
NN/g · Baymard
Recommendation
  • Derive crumbs from NAV_GROUPS + a per-route resolveCrumb(id) so the last crumb is "SL-2026-0106 · Roy".
  • Drop the app-name root; the sidebar is the home link. Top-level pages need no breadcrumb at all (the H1 is the location).
  • Mobile: "‹ Dossiers" up-one-level crumb in the header.
Same source, five surfacesSidebar label, breadcrumb, PageHeader title, workspace tab, document.title. One string, defined once.

The stepper shows position, not progress

Impact · highEffort · medium
Observed"Past" = earlier in the array than the step you scrolled to, not "completed". Stamps come from keyword-matching the workflow log, and only steps 1, 4, 6, 7 have keywords — steps 9, 11, 10, 8 can never show who/when. Eight labels of 2–4 words in one row overflow at 1440 px (the screenshot clips step 7; step 8 is off-screen and undiscoverable). Numbers restart at 1 even though sections are ids 1/4/6/9/11/10/7/8.
EvidenceGOV.UK task-list (8 research rounds): each task shows Not started / In progress / Completed / Cannot start yet / Problem; completed is de-emphasised, actionable states are tagged, blocked steps aren't links, the whole row is the control, and users may complete tasks in any order. Carbon progress indicator: 1–2-word labels, helper-text slot under each step, interactive variant for jumping. NN/g: strict wizards are wrong for "users who perform tasks repeatedly or possess domain expertise" — allow skip-ahead and loop-back. USWDS in-page nav: IntersectionObserver, focus moves to the section heading on click.
GOV.UK · Carbon · NN/g · USWDS
Recommendation
  • Compute a real status per step from dossier data (planifications by type, accord slots filled, rapport generated, note d'honoraire present): À faire · En cours · Terminé · Bloqué.
  • Render: ✓ muted for Terminé, filled accent for the active step, outline for À faire, grey non-link for Bloqué (with a tooltip saying why).
  • Helper text = stamp "Y. Naji · 30/08 14:12" taken from the same data, not from log keywords.
  • Labels: Mission · Visite avant · Accord · Visite en cours · 2ᵉ accord · Visite après · Rapport · Honoraires.
  • Below xl: the row scrolls with a fade edge and the active step auto-centres; above 2xl consider a vertical rail on the left (Carbon vertical variant) which never clips.
  • Add "Tout déplier / Tout replier" (NN/g accordion guidance) and default critical sections open.
Doubles asthe data source for Suivi d'équipe (on-time / overdue / not completed per step), which today computes the same thing separately.

A full grid, wrapping identifiers and a red trash on every row

Impact · mediumEffort · small–medium

08

Feedback, loading & motion

Instant-feeling navigation is mostly configuration

Impact · mediumEffort · small
Observedloading.tsx exists on 4 of 19 routes; the rest fall to a full-screen PageLoader or nothing. transition-all on the sidebar inset and buttons; html { scroll-behavior: smooth } globally with no reduced-motion guard; dashboard cards stagger-fade with inline delays; no view transitions; no route-change focus management.
EvidenceNext.js prefetches dynamic routes only up to the nearest loading.js boundary, so a missing loading file means no instant shell. NN/g: < 1 s no indicator; 2–10 s skeleton for route loads; frame-only skeletons are spinner-equivalent. Vercel WIG: animate only transform/opacity, never transition: all, honour prefers-reduced-motion. Next.js view-transition guide: exit 150 ms, enter 210 ms, disable under reduced motion. Material 3: 100–200 ms for controls, 250–300 ms for panels; enter decelerate, exit accelerate.
Next.js · NN/g · Vercel WIG · View Transitions
Recommendation
  • A loading.tsx per route mirroring its PageHeader + primary block (copy the Dossiers one).
  • Replace transition-all with explicit properties; sidebar collapse 200–250 ms decelerate.
  • Guard smooth scroll with @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference).
  • Optional: React <ViewTransition> cross-fade of the page body only (shell stays static) — 150/210 ms.
  • Toasts for passive confirmations only; errors inline with role="alert" (NN/g: toast is "inappropriate for error messages").
PerceptionWith prefetch + skeletons that match the layout, navigation between list and dossier feels like a tab switch rather than a page load.

Mobile shell for field agents

A hamburger, an empty header and a floating help button

Impact · high (for Agents de Terrain)Effort · medium
ObservedBelow md the sidebar becomes a Sheet behind a hamburger; the header shows only that trigger. The missions page already has a good card list and Avant/En cours/Après tabs, but its filters, actions and the camera flow have no fixed home; a help FAB overlaps content bottom-right.
EvidenceNN/g (n = 179): hidden nav halves usage and adds 15 % task time on mobile. Material 3 navigation bar: 3–5 destinations, 24 dp icons, 56×32 dp active pill, labels always visible for ≤ 3 items; FAB = "the single most common action on a screen", bottom-end; bottom sheets for filters and choices; list items 56/72/88 dp. Apple HIG: 3–5 tabs, never a "More" tab. Hoober: ~75 % of touches are thumb-driven, so primary controls belong at the bottom.

Visual system — how "modern SaaS" is actually achieved

The locked direction (warm cream + teal) is good and distinctive. What separates it from Linear, Stripe or Attio is not the palette but budgeting: how much accent, how much border, how much weight. These are token-level changes that every page inherits.

DimensionTodayProven practice → change
Accent budgetTeal on every heading (h1–h6 { color: heading-fg }), active nav fill, links, ref column, badges, buttons, FAB.Linear/Stripe: the accent appears on the primary button, active nav, focus ring and links — and almost nowhere else. Headings return to ink; ref column to ink + mono; teal earns attention again. Linear DESIGN.md · Stripe DESIGN.md
Neutral ladderbackground, card, muted, border, plus an ad-hoc heading-bg beige band.Radix 12-step semantics: surface-0/1/2 (page, card, hover), hairline (step 6), hairline-strong (step 7–8), text-muted (11), text (12). Retire the beige band; table headers and section headers use surface-2. Radix scale
DepthSidebar shadow-xl, header shadow + blur, cards shadow-sm → md on hover, full-grid table borders.Borders over shadows: 1 px hairlines and surface steps carry hierarchy; shadows only on popovers/dialogs (2–3 tiny stacked layers). "Structure should be felt not seen." Linear · Vercel Geist
Type pairingOutfit (a geometric display face) for everything, 10 px labels in the sidebar.Keep Outfit for H1/H2 and KPI values; use a text face for UI (Inter, IBM Plex Sans or Source Sans 3) at 13–14 px with tabular-nums on figures; floor 12 px. Carbon productive: body 14/18–20, label 12/16, heading-03 20/28, heading-04 28/36. Carbon · Stripe (tnum)

Dark modeFull palette; enableSystem=false, default light; sidebar toggle.Keep the palette; enable system preference; move the toggle into the user menu; desaturate the teal one notch on dark (already done at 172 50% 55 %). Material dark theme

Why this reads as "modern": Linear, Vercel, Attio and Stripe's dashboards all share the same recipe — one accent used sparingly, a neutral ladder instead of shadows, 13–14 px UI type with tabular figures, 6–8 px radii, and hairline structure. The cream-and-teal identity stays; only the budget changes.
